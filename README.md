# Olist-Brazilian-e-commerce-analysis
Olist is a Brazilian marketplace that connects thousands of sellers with customers throughout Brazil. The platform collects information on orders, customers, products, payments, deliveries, and customer reviews.
The goal of the project is to analyze sales data and identify factors affecting revenue, delivery efficiency, and customer satisfaction. The project focuses on analyzing key performance indicators (KPIs) and answering business questions.
The dataset contains real data from the Olist platform for the years 2016–2018 and includes over 100,000 orders as well as information on customers, products, payments, deliveries, and customer reviews.
